Hotel Cancellation Policy

Cancellation & Refund Policy

Guests may cancel free of charge up to 48 hours before check-in. After this period, a cancellation fee of one night’s stay will be charged. Refunds will be processed within 7–10 business days.

Payment Policy

Full payment or an advance deposit is required to confirm reservations. We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, and online transfers. Pending payments must be cleared at check-in.

Last-Minute Cancellation Policy

Cancellations made within 24 hours of check-in will incur a charge of 100% of the first night’s stay. No refunds will be provided for same-day cancellations.

Non-Refundable Policy

Bookings under the non-refundable rate plan cannot be modified or cancelled. The full booking amount will be charged, and no refund will be issued.
